Watermark guides

Learn how to put a day count on photos and use the same starting date to document plants and fitness progress.

Document growth and progress

How to add a Day N watermark to photos on iPhone

Choose a Day 1 date in Timestamp Camera. The watermark shows the day number when you take a new photo.

How to photograph plant growth from sowing to first leaves

Set the sowing date as Day 1 to see when a seed sprouted and developed its first true leaves. Keep the camera angle steady so the photos are easier to compare.

How to take weekly fitness progress photos with a day count

Take each photo from the same spot with the same framing and lighting. A day-count watermark shows when each photo falls in your training plan.
