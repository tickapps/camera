Timestamp Camera - Time & GPS+

Timestamp Camera - Time & GPS+ is a free timestamp camera app that adds real-time date, time, GPS location, map, and address watermarks to your photos and videos.

Choose what your photos and videos show

  • Location: GPS coordinates (latitude & longitude), full or short address, altitude, location accuracy, and bearing.
  • Embedded map: a live map watermark in Standard, Satellite, or Hybrid mode.
  • Date & time: customizable formats (e.g. yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm:ss) and time zone display (e.g. GMT+8).
  • Temperature & pressure: add temperature and atmospheric pressure to supported watermarks.

On iOS, the Plant Growth Tracking template also supports humidity. Available elements vary by platform and template.

Every watermark element can be turned on or off independently. Font, font size, and color are adjustable for each element. Custom location tags can be saved and reused.

Watermark templates by platform

iOS and Android both offer the following six watermark templates. Available elements can vary by platform and template.

Android

  • Default — choose date, time, address, GPS coordinates, altitude, accuracy, bearing, temperature, atmospheric pressure, and map elements.
  • Accent — a compact layout with an editable badge, date and time, time zone, address, and an optional accent line.
  • Work Record — a work-photo layout with an editable badge and selectable coordinates, date and time, time zone, location icon, and Verified label.
  • Verified Stamp — a stamp-style layout with address, date and time, time zone, and optional location icon and Verified label.
  • Plant Growth Tracking — a template for recording plant growth over time.
  • Inspection Record — add people, notes, and weather conditions such as sunny or cloudy to inspection records.

iOS

  • Default — choose date, time, address, GPS coordinates, altitude, accuracy, bearing, temperature, atmospheric pressure, and map elements.
  • Accent — a compact layout with an editable badge, date and time, time zone, address, and an optional accent line.
  • Work Record — a work-photo layout with an editable badge and selectable coordinates, date and time, time zone, location icon, and Verified label.
  • Verified Stamp — a stamp-style layout with address, date and time, time zone, and optional location icon and Verified label.
  • Plant Growth Tracking — a template for recording plant growth over time.
  • Inspection Record — add people, notes, and weather conditions such as sunny or cloudy to inspection records.

All watermark templates are free to use, with no ad to watch before switching. The app still displays ads. Template availability can change with the installed app version.

Photo reports on iOS

After you take at least one photo in the app, the Report button appears next to the shutter button.

  • Combine 1–42 images into one shareable report image, arranged three per row.
  • Use watermarked photos taken in the app or images from your photo library. Existing watermarks are preserved; importing an image does not add a watermark.
  • Add your logo, company/project, scene name, reporter, and date.
  • Reports are generated locally on your device, and the feature is free.

A photo report combines images; it is separate from the watermark templates used when capturing photos and videos. Reports are not saved inside the app, and each new report starts fresh.

Frequently asked questions

Is Timestamp Camera - Time & GPS+ free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no subscription and no in-app purchases. The app is supported by ads.

What information can it stamp on photos?

Date and time, time zone, GPS coordinates, altitude, accuracy, bearing, full or short address, an embedded map (Standard, Satellite, or Hybrid), temperature, and atmospheric pressure. Humidity is also available in the iOS Plant Growth Tracking template; available elements vary by platform and template.

Can I add GPS and timestamp to photos for work?

Yes. The app is built for work documentation - construction, property inspections, delivery records, and field services. The Work Record and Inspection Record templates are designed for these cases.

Does it work on videos?

Yes. Watermarks are applied in real time to both photos and videos.

Can it track plant growth?

Yes. On iOS, the Plant Growth Tracking template counts the days since your set Day 1 date and stamps the day count, your note, date, time, temperature, humidity, and location on each photo.

Which features need an internet connection?

Maps and address lookup need an internet connection. If a field does not load, check your network connection and location permission.
