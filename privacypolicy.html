Timestamp Camera - Time & GPS Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective date: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Timestamp Camera - Time & GPS ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the App and this website. The App is designed to help you capture photos with automatic timestamps, GPS information, and other optional overlays while giving you control over what is added to each image.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We collect only the information needed to provide and improve the App's core features.

(1) Photos You Choose to Capture or Import

  • When you take a photo with the App or choose an image from your photo library, we access only the photo you select.
  • These photos are used to add timestamps, GPS details, notes, or other overlays that you choose.
  • Your photos are processed on your device. We do not upload your photos to our servers just for normal use of the App.

(2) Camera Permission

  • We request camera access only when you choose to take photos inside the App.
  • If you deny camera permission, you can still use other functions that do not require taking new photos inside the App.

(3) Location Permission

  • If you enable GPS or location-based stamps, we request access to your device location so the App can add location information to your photos.
  • Location access is optional. If you deny it, you can still use timestamp features without GPS data.
  • We do not use location permission for continuous background tracking.

(4) Photo Library or Storage Permission

  • We request access to your photo library or storage only so you can choose photos to edit and save the images you create.
  • We do not scan, collect, or analyze your entire photo library.

(5) Device, Usage, and Diagnostic Information

  • To keep the App stable and improve performance, we may collect limited diagnostic and usage information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app interactions, crash logs, and approximate network information.
  • This information helps us troubleshoot issues, understand feature usage, prevent abuse, and improve the App experience.

(6) Advertising and Analytics

  • Because the App may be offered free of charge, we may use analytics and advertising services to support operation and improve the product.
  • These services may collect device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, advertising interaction data, and app usage events according to their own privacy policies.

2. How Timestamp and GPS Features Work

  • The App automatically adds date and time information based on your device settings and the moment a photo is taken or edited.
  • If you enable GPS features, the App can add location-based information to the photo output you choose to save.
  • You control whether timestamp, GPS data, notes, or other overlays appear on the final image.
  • If you do not want location details embedded in a photo, you can turn off location-related features before saving or sharing the image.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the limited situations below:

  • With service providers that help us operate analytics, diagnostics, and advertising features.
  • When required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid government request.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, the public, or the security and integrity of the App.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of some or all of our business, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.

4. Data Retention

  • We generally do not store your photos on our servers for ordinary App use.
  • Information collected for analytics, diagnostics, fraud prevention, or advertising may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, subject to applicable law and our service providers' retention practices.
  • When information is no longer needed, we will delete it, anonymize it, or ensure it is retained only as required by law.

5. Your Choices and Controls

  • You can choose whether to enable camera, photo library, or location permissions through your device settings.
  • You can decide whether GPS information or other overlays are included in exported images.
  • You can limit ad tracking through your device privacy settings where supported by your platform.
  • You may stop using the App at any time and remove it from your device.

6. Security

  • We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
  • No system can guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect the information we handle and to reduce risk.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and address the situation.

8. International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate contractual, organizational, and legal measures designed to protect the information involved in accordance with applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or improvements to our privacy practices. When we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
